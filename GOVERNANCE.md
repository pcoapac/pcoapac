# Governance 治理
- **Admins 管理员** run the servers and have final say on safety and legal issues. 负责服务器，对安全和法律问题有最终决定权。
- **Moderators 版主** (volunteers, 1–2 per category) keep discussions on topic and handle flags. 志愿者，维护讨论秩序。
- **Maintainers 维护者** review pull requests in this repo. 审核本仓库的 PR。
- **Decisions 决策**: proposals go to the forum Community category; major changes are decided by a community poll open for at least 7 days. 重大变更在 Community 板块发提案，投票至少 7 天。
- **Sponsorship disclosure 赞助说明**: servers are currently sponsored by a team from a pest control products company. Sponsors have no editorial control; vendor posts must be disclosed and stay in Products & Equipment. 服务器目前由一家虫控产品企业的团队赞助；赞助方不干预内容，厂商发帖须表明身份并只发在「产品与设备」板块。
