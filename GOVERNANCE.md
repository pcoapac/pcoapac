# Community Governance

PCOAPAC is built on transparency, neutrality, and shared community stewardship.

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

* **Admins**: Responsible for server infrastructure, database backups, legal compliance, and community safety.
* **Moderators**: Community volunteers who keep discussions constructive, ensure topics remain in appropriate categories, and review flagged posts.
* **Maintainers**: Open-source volunteers who review and merge Pull Requests in this repository.
* **Verified Pros**: Certified pest control technicians, licensed company operators, and entomologists recognized by the community.

---

## Decision-Making Process

* **Proposals**: All major architectural, category, or policy proposals are submitted to the **Community** category on the forum.
* **Community Polls**: Substantial changes require an open community poll lasting at least 7 days before implementation.
* **Sponsorship Disclosure**: Server infrastructure is sponsored by a pest management technology team. Sponsors hold zero editorial control. Commercial vendor announcements must be transparently disclosed and restricted exclusively to the **Products & Equipment** category.
